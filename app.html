How To Use XE3AGLE

Simple guide — read this before your first session

What is XE3AGLE?

XE3AGLE is your trading checklist app. Before you take a trade, it makes sure you have followed all your rules. If you haven't — it says NO. If you have — it says TRADE APPROVED.

Think of it like a pilot's pre-flight checklist. A pilot doesn't take off until every box is ticked. You don't trade until every condition is met.

Plan the trade. Execute the plan. Stop when the plan says stop.
What You Do Every Day — In Order

Follow these steps every day. Do them in order. Do not skip any.

1
Open XE3AGLE first
Open this app before you open your charts. Every time. No exceptions.
2
Go to Prepare (tap Prepare in the menu)
Pick how you feel right now. Be honest. Then read your rules and tap "I Accept Today's Rules". You cannot trade until you do this.
3
Go to Setup & Analysis (tap Setup in the menu)
Look at your charts. Fill in every section: Bias, Liquidity, Sweep, MSS, Entry Model, Entry price, Stop Loss, Take Profit. Then tap Save Analysis.
4
Go to Pre-Trade Check (tap Pre-Trade Check in the menu)
The app checks everything for you automatically. If you see TRADE APPROVED — you can trade. If you see NO TRADE — fix the items marked ✗ first.
5
Start the trade
Tap Confirm & Start Trade. Then go to your broker and place the trade exactly as you planned. Do not change anything.
6
Wait
Go back to the Today page. Watch the trade. Do not move your stop loss. Do not add more money to the trade. Just wait.
7
Close the trade
When the trade is done, tap Win, Loss, or Breakeven on the Today page. Type in how much you made or lost. The app saves everything automatically.
8
Review your day
When trading is over for the day, fill in the Daily Review at the bottom of the Today page. Write what you learned. Close the charts.
What Each Page Does
Today
Your main screen. Shows your daily profit/loss, how many trades you've taken, your current status, and controls for the active trade. Start here every day.
Prepare
Pick your emotion and accept the rules. Until you do this, the app will not approve any trade. It takes 30 seconds.
Setup & Analysis
Where you describe your trade. Fill in your bias, the liquidity levels, whether you saw a sweep and a market structure shift, your entry model, and your exact prices. The risk calculator shows you how much money is at risk.
Pre-Trade Check
The gate. The app checks every condition. Green tick = passed. Red cross = failed. All ticks = TRADE APPROVED. Any cross = NO TRADE. Fix the red ones, then come back.
Journal
Every trade you've ever taken is saved here. You can view details, edit a trade, copy it, or delete it. You can also add trades manually.
Stats
Your performance numbers — win rate, total profit/loss, best setup, which emotions lead to better trades, and charts showing your progress over time.
Psychology
8 cards about the mental mistakes traders make — greed, revenge, FOMO and more. Tap any card to read the full explanation and a reminder phrase. Read these when you feel off.
Status
Shows your current session status and all active limits. Also has the Kill Switch — tap it to immediately stop trading for the day if you feel you are not in the right headspace.
Settings
Change your account balance, risk %, daily loss limit, max trades, and more. Also where you back up and restore your data.
When Does Trading Stop Automatically?

The app will block all new trades and show TRADING TERMINATED if any of these happen:

You lost too much today
Default: if your account is down 1% or more for the day.
You lost 2 trades in a row
Default: 2 consecutive losses. This stops you from revenge trading.
You hit your daily profit target
Default: +2R. You made your money. Stop. Do not give it back.
You took the maximum number of trades
Default: 3 trades per day. Fewer, better trades beats more, worse trades.
You pressed the Kill Switch
You can stop yourself at any time. Go to Status → tap Kill Switch.
You can change all of these limits in Settings to match your own trading plan.
What Does "R" Mean?

R = the amount you risked on one trade.

Example: if your account is $10,000 and you risk 0.5%, you risk $50 per trade. That means 1R = $50.

+2R You made double your risk — great trade. That is +$100.
+1R You made exactly your risk amount back. That is +$50.
0R Breakeven. You got out with no profit and no loss.
-1R You lost your risk amount. Stop loss was hit. That is -$50.

When you close a trade, the app asks you to type the actual R. If you won but only got +1.5R instead of the full +2R, type 1.5. The app does the dollar math for you.

What to Fill In on the Setup Page
Bias
Is the market going up or down? Pick Bullish (up), Bearish (down), or Neutral. Do this for both the big picture and the smaller timeframe you trade on. If they agree — good. If they disagree — be careful.
Liquidity
Where are the price levels where lots of buy/sell orders are sitting? Tick the ones you can see — previous highs, previous lows, equal highs, equal lows.
Sweep
Did price briefly move past one of those levels and then come back? That is a sweep. Tick the box only if you actually saw it happen on your chart.
MSS (Market Structure Shift)
After the sweep, did the market change direction? A market structure shift means price broke through a key level in the opposite direction. This is your signal that the move is real. Tick the box only when you see it clearly.
Entry Model
What pattern are you using to enter? Pick one: FVG (a gap in price), Order Block (a key candle), OTE (a Fibonacci retracement), or Other.
Entry, Stop Loss, Take Profit
Type in the exact price for each one from your chart. The risk calculator will immediately show you how much money you are risking and what your reward could be.
Saving Your Data

All your trades and settings are saved inside your browser. Nothing goes to the internet. But if you clear your browser data, you will lose everything.

Back up regularly. Go to Settings → Export Backup. This downloads a file with all your data. Keep it safe. If you ever need to restore, go to Settings → Import Backup and pick the file.

Do not clear your browser data or switch browsers without exporting a backup first. There is no recovery if you do not have a backup file.
